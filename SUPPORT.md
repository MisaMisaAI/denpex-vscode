# Denpex support

- Product support and sales: [denpex.com/contact](https://denpex.com/contact)
- Documentation: [denpex.com/docs](https://denpex.com/docs)
- Service health: [denpex.com/status](https://denpex.com/status)
- Security reports: [denpex.com/security/disclosure](https://denpex.com/security/disclosure)

When reporting a diagnosis issue, include the Denpex engine version and failure type shown
in the report. Do not send customer logs, credentials, model data, or other sensitive
content unless your organization has approved that disclosure.
